✦ Clarity & Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working together, turnaround times, and file delivery.

What types of projects do you edit and direct?

+

I specialize in high-end social video editing (Reels, TikTok, Shorts), brand storytelling films, lookbook trailers, product showcases, and kinetic motion graphics for tech & lifestyle brands.

What is your typical turnaround time?

+

For social shorts and reels, first cuts are typically delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Larger commercial cuts or multi-asset campaigns take 3 to 5 business days, with clear milestones at every step.

Do you handle color grading and sound design?

+

Yes. Every edit includes custom color grading (film curves, exposure balancing, highlight rolloff) and layered sound design (foley, transitions, ambient room tone, and music mastering).

How do we send raw footage and collaborate?

+

You can share files via Google Drive, Dropbox, MASV, or WeTransfer. Review drafts are shared via Frame.io or private review links with timestamped feedback for effortless iterations.

Can you export content for multiple aspect ratios?

+

Absolutely. One project run can deliver 9:16 vertical for TikTok & Reels, 16:9 for YouTube & web headers, and 1:1 or 4:5 for Instagram feeds, all formatted and optimized without awkward cropping.
