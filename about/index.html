Campaigns, lookbooks and kinetic motion at scale

A dedicated visual and video studio for brands: campaign imagery, lookbooks and social cuts from your own concepts. No shoot day, no agency drag, no compromises.

What types of projects do you edit and direct? +

I specialize in high-impact social video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts), brand storytelling films, lookbook trailers, product showcases, and kinetic motion graphics for lifestyle & tech brands.

What is your typical turnaround time? +

For social shorts and reels, first cuts are typically delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Larger commercial cuts or multi-asset campaigns take 3 to 5 business days, with clear milestones at every step.

Do you handle color grading and sound design? +

Yes. Every project includes custom color grading (35mm film emulation, exposure balancing, highlight rolloff) and layered sound design (foley, transitions, ambient room tone, and music mastering).

Does any key visual become motion as well as stills? +

Yes. Any campaign image can be developed into short-form motion for Reels, TikTok and web banners, keeping the exact same color science, subject, and atmosphere as the still. No separate video day.

How do we collaborate and send raw footage? +

You can share footage via Google Drive, Dropbox, MASV, or WeTransfer. Review drafts are delivered via Frame.io with timestamped commenting for seamless feedback.
