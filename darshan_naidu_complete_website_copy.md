# Darshan Naidu - Complete Website Copy
### A minimal, personal portfolio for video editing, visual design & social media

**Website:** https://darshannaidu.vercel.app/  
**Purpose:** Replace the current agency-focused copy with a personal creative portfolio that feels natural, confident, simple, and human.

## The voice

The writing should feel like Darshan talking about his work - not a marketing agency describing itself.

- Keep sentences short and conversational.
- Let the work do the talking. Avoid big claims like “transforming the world,” “visionary,” “revolutionary,” “world-class,” or “cutting-edge.”
- Use **I / my** when talking about Darshan personally. Avoid **we / our team / our studio** unless a real team is involved.
- Prefer clear descriptions over buzzwords.
- Don't claim results, clients, years of experience, or services unless they're true.
- Use small moments of personality, but don't force jokes into every section.
- Keep headings short enough to work with the website's oversized typography and animations.
