Overview

Capturing India’s architectural wonders through cinematic color harmony, monumental geometry, and timeless print aesthetics.

India Heritage is a curated five-piece visual retrospective celebrating iconic architectural landmarks across the subcontinent. From the dawn mist of Agra's Taj Mahal to the intricate latticework of Jaipur's Hawa Mahal, the nocturnal golden radiance of Amritsar's Golden Temple, the nostalgic streetscapes of Kolkata, and the soaring sandstone of Delhi's Qutub Minar.

Each composition balances mid-century travel poster tradition with contemporary digital color science — pairing bold typographic hierarchies with evocative atmospheric depth.

Disciplines

01 / Agra
Taj Mahal — Symphonic Proportion & Dawn Stillness

The Taj Mahal composition focuses on symmetrical purity and serene horizon lines. Soft morning light bathes the ivory marble dome, framed by quiet reflections and muted earthen tones that give the poster an eternal, transcendent mood.

02 / Hawa Mahal — Jaipur
03 / Harmandir Sahib — Amritsar
02 & 03 / Heritage Studies
Terracotta Harmony & Golden Reverence

Hawa Mahal honors the intricate latticework of Jaipur's 953 honeycomb casements, capturing warm terracotta tones bathed in desert sunlight.

Golden Temple depicts the spiritual majesty of Sri Harmandir Sahib floating upon the sacred Amrit Sarovar under a nocturnal indigo sky, its gilded sanctum glowing with peaceful radiance.

04 / Heritage — Kolkata
05 / Qutub Minar — New Delhi
04 & 05 / Urban & Monumental
Cultural Rhythm & Soaring Monumentality

Kolkata brings to life the timeless character of the City of Joy — iconic yellow Ambassador taxis, electric tram tracks, and historic colonial facades rich with warmth and movement.

Qutub Minar captures the dramatic verticality of Delhi’s 73-meter fluted minaret, celebrating ancient stone carvings, banded inscriptions, and geometric strength against a crisp skyline.
