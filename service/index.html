Nothing complicated. Just a good process.

How we work
A simple, collaborative way to take something from rough starting point to finished creative work.

01 - Talk it through

We start with what you need, who it's for, and what you want it to feel like. References are welcome; a rough idea or folder of clips is enough to begin.

02 - Make a first version

I put the edit or design together, try a few directions where needed, and focus on getting the main idea and rhythm right before polishing the details.

03 - Fine-tune it

We review the work, make the agreed changes, and get the final files exported and ready for the formats or platforms you need.
